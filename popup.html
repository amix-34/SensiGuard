⚖️ Malaysian law: PDPA 2010 (amended 2024)

Organisations must protect personal data from misuse, loss and unauthorised access. This is the Security Principle. The law mostly binds companies, so users have few tools of their own.

SensiGuard helps you reduce the risk of exposing your own data. It does not make a company PDPA compliant.
